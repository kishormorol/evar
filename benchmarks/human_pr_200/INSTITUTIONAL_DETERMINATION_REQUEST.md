## Purpose and procedures

The project evaluates whether language-model code-review claims are supported by exact
repository evidence. Source material comprises public GitHub pull-request comments and
public repository snapshots. Two independently recruited software experts will each
review the same 682-item identity-blinded queue. They will determine eligibility,
normalize a technical claim, assign a frozen claim family, and judge whether the claim
is supported at the reviewed snapshot and absent at the merged snapshot. A third person
will adjudicate only disagreements after the two independent exports are frozen.

The task does not ask experts to disclose personal experiences, sensitive attributes,
health information, employment evaluations, or private communications. Experts will
not be ranked or profiled. Expected completion time and compensation, if any, must be
inserted in the information sheet before recruitment.

## Data handling

- Experts use stable pseudonymous identifiers in exported files.
- The identity-to-identifier mapping, acknowledgments, compensation records, and contact
  information remain in a private study log outside the public repository.
- Public source usernames and URLs are retained only where needed for provenance.
- Publications report aggregate agreement, yield, timing, and benchmark statistics.
- Advisory model annotations are withheld from experts and cannot become ground truth.
- A reasonable source-removal request will be logged and assessed by the study lead.

## Risks and benefits

Foreseeable risks are limited to ordinary task burden, possible professional discomfort
when judging ambiguous code, and confidentiality risk around reviewer identity. The
protocol mitigates these risks through voluntary participation, withdrawal before an
export is incorporated, pseudonymous identifiers, aggregate reporting, and restricted
access to the private identity map. There is no promised direct benefit to reviewers.
The anticipated research benefit is a more reproducible evaluation of automated code
review.
