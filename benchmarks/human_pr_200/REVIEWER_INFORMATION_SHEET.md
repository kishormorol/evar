You are invited to independently assess public code-review examples for a research
benchmark. You will view a public review comment and two frozen public repository

claim, choose one of five fixed categories, and judge the claim at both snapshots.

## Independence and blinding

Final experts work independently and must not discuss individual items until both
exports validate. You will not see another expert's answers, model-generated advisory
labels, protocol names, verifier outcomes, or benchmark labels. The adjudicator sees
identity-blinded disagreements only.

## Data and privacy

Use only the pseudonymous reviewer identifier supplied by the study lead. Do not put
your name, email address, employer, or other identifying information in an export. The
study lead stores the identity mapping and any compensation record privately. Reports
use aggregate agreement and timing and do not rank individual reviewers.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time before your completed export is
validated and incorporated by contacting [study contact]. After the de-identified
resolved benchmark is frozen, removing an individual decision may be impracticable;
the study contact will explain the applicable institutional rule.
